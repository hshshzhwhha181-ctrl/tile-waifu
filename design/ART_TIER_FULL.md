# Art tier FULL: the visual spec for every heroine image

Status: **owner decision, 2026-11-28.** Yandex Games is dropped, so there is no SFW / dual-build constraint any more. Tier FULL is THE art direction for the RuStore / APK 18+ build and for every heroine batch (H0 to H4). There is no "Standard" or "Neutral" fallback tier in the art packages; if a store ever needs a calmer build it is a separate later pass, not a tier of this spec.

Level: the very top of the 17+ / 18+ suggestive range that Brown Dust 2, Azur Lane and Nikke ship (round 2, owner order 2026-11-28: maximum fan-service up to the ceiling in section 3b). Style class only; no character, costume, pose or asset is copied from any of them.

Written by P50b (round 2: body table in section 3a, boldness ceiling in section 3b, hero splash poses, supersede banners on the old rules). Prompts and wardrobe tables in this document are generated from `tools/art/full_tier_wardrobe.py` and `tools/art/build_art_batches.py`, and the first Full-tier frames are in `design/golden_full/` (see `REPORT.md` there). Nothing here is approved or registered; the owner approves.

## 1. Why the P50 golden composites came out tame (root cause)

The P50 frames (`design/golden/contact_sheet.jpg`) were not a model limit. They were art-directed as the cautious tier:

- `lora-v2/SPEC_V2.md` section 2, "dataset policy for v2 (stricter intersection, so one LoRA serves both builds)": no bikini or swimwear, no bunny costume, no garter or lace-top stocking visible, no navel or torso cut-out windows, no sheer cloth over the torso, slits at or below mid-thigh, legwear = opaque tights or opaque thigh-high socks, no pleated mini skirts, no leg-raised poses in skirts. That policy was written so ONE style LoRA could serve both the Yandex build and the RuStore build.
- `docs/HEROINES.md` section 4 red lines (forbidden: lying down, all fours, arched back, sitting on a table, legs toward the camera, pulling collar or hem; "in prompts write only opaque tights and never stockings") and `docs/LEGWEAR.md` 1.1 (garter belt, lingerie, panties, upskirt never written; band or lace at the thigh only through a slit and not above mid-thigh) were written for the Yandex Games build.
- The P59 batch prompts (`H0_golden_composites.json` and the other H files) inherited all of that and added recipe v4's `form-fitting ... fully opaque fabric, garment stays exactly in place ... no loud bokeh ... matte skin ... no AI gloss`, a muted background and a "calm confident smile, hands lightly together" pose.
- The owner's decisions already allowed more for the RuStore build (HEROINES header, 2026-11-23 two tiers, 2026-11-24 target Mature 17+ / 18+): stockings and thigh-highs with lace tops, garters as part of a costume, bodysuits and swimsuits, deep slits and necklines, open back, mini skirts in an adult context, flirty poses, glance over the shoulder, maid / bartender / idol / evening outfits. P50 never used that room.

So the P50 frames were the Standard tier by construction. Tier FULL removes the Yandex intersection and uses the owner's allowance in full.

## 2. Hard lines (every build, not negotiable)

These did not change. The validator enforces the word-level part (`tools/art/validate_batches.py`, `HARD_BANNED_FULL`).

1. **Every character is clearly adult.** Adult face (mature features, adult makeup, no childlike roundness or large-eye infantilisation) and a grown woman's figure; heights and head counts differ per heroine (section 3a) but the head count is never below **6.75** and never above 8.0; age 20+ in canon and profile (all ten are 23 to 27). Petite means a shorter adult woman, never a young-looking one. "Young face plus revealing outfit" is forbidden.
2. **No nudity.** No visible nipples or areolae, no genitals, no anatomical groin outline, no uncovered buttocks beyond what a high-cut bikini or leotard bottom covers. Garments and legwear always cover chest, hips and groin and stay in place; the bold part is cut, fabric and pose, not exposure. A plunging neckline, a heart-shaped chest cutout, a keyhole front, an open back and a high slit are allowed only when fabric edges, trim or a closure still cover the nipples and everything below the waist.
3. **No sexual acts, no fluids**, no implied penetration, no sexualised violence, no bondage, no clothing destruction, no undressing as a reward.
4. **No school or minor coding.** No school uniform, seifuku, sailor collar, school skirt (including pleated mini skirts as a school look), classroom, desk, teacher-student, schoolbag, loafers with white knee socks, gingham, childlike props or accessories, and no "student", "teen", "kid", "minor" wording.
5. **No text, letters, numerals, logos or watermark in art.** Mahjong tiles blank or absent. (The owner's UI mock-ups 12 to 15 carry baked text and numerals: they are concept only.)
6. **Camera.** No under-the-skirt angle, no fly-over or body-part scanning, no isolated jiggle as the point of the frame. Tilt up at most 10 degrees on skirts. Eye-level to slightly high is the default.
7. **One character per frame** (no duo frames in this tier), no alcohol in hands (tea, cocktail shakers as props are fine), flirt is directed at the player only.

Vocabulary rule for the generator: fashion and pin-up words only. Never write `sexy`, `cleavage`, `busty`, `thicc`, `lingerie`, `panties`, `upskirt`, `nude`, `naked`, `nipple`, `topless`, `underboob`, `sideboob` (the fashion terms are `underbust cut-out` and `side-cut`, section 3b). Positive constraints only (the model has no negative prompt; the recipe ends with "Garments and legwear cover the chest, hips and groin and stay exactly in place").

## 3b. Boldness ceiling (round 2, owner order 2026-11-28)

Target: the poses and outfits of Brown Dust 2 and Azur Lane splash art, maximum fan-service for an adult audience. This is the ceiling; the hard lines of section 2 are the wall. Moderation test (P50b rounds 2 and 3: 12 bold-pose probes, 2 lineup images, 10 height re-rolls): the generator accepted all of the below with fashion wording, 0 refusals. The most exposed probe (`golden_full/round2/boldness_probes/kohaku_swim_H1.jpg`: string bikini, wet-look sheer sarong, kneeling arched in an onsen) is the practical ceiling: triangle cups and bottoms still cover everything, so anything bolder than that is out.

**Cut and fabric (all allowed, garments still cover the nipples and the groin):**

- `underbust cut-out` (fashion term for the underboob window): a window under the bust line of a bodysuit, leotard, qipao or dress, edged in trim; the bust itself is fully covered by lined fabric above it.
- `side-cut` (the sideboob term): high-cut sides of a leotard or bodice that show the flank; the front panel still covers the chest.
- High-cut leotards and bodysuits (hips cut high, lined fabric over the groin, `thigh straps`), tight bodysuits and latex-look fabric, bunny-style leotards, armour leotard-dresses.
- String-style bikinis: thin tie sides and neck, triangle cups and bottoms that cover the nipples and the groin completely and are opaque; with an optional wet-look sheer shirt or sarong over them.
- Thigh straps, garters, garter chains, ribbon-and-bell bands, lace-top stockings, fishnet, scale-pattern stockings.
- Sheer and wet-look fabric: robes, sarongs, overlays, sleeves and legwear; always over a lined garment, never over a bare chest or groin. Wet or sweat glow on skin, hair and fabric.
- Sleepwear: satin mini nightgowns under silk robe-dresses (the word `lingerie` is never written), pillow hugging, bed and daybed scenes.
- Bath, onsen and steam scenes in robes, bikinis, swimsuits or towels that stay covering; steam and water as cover, not as a reveal.

**Poses (all allowed):** reclining and sprawled on a bed or daybed (on the back or the side, one knee up or an arm trailing), lying on the side propped on an elbow, kneeling with an arched back, both hands in the hair, stretching with the arms overhead, leaning forward toward the viewer, hand on hip, hip tilt, leg lifted behind, crossed legs, straddling a chair backwards (in leotards, shorts, bodysuits or trousers; the legs not opened toward the camera), pillow hugging, close-up teasing and flushed faces, sipping tea with a glance, and **back-view and hip-focused three-quarter angles in tight or short outfits** (eye level, looking back over the shoulder; the camera never goes below the waist and never looks up a skirt).

**Not used (hard):** on all fours, legs open toward the camera, squats with open knees (a squat is drawn in three-quarter view with the knees together), under-the-skirt or from-below angles, a body-part scan by the camera, any frame whose point is an exposed nipple or groin.

## 6. Prompt recipe (tested)

Tail `v5f` (in `build_art_batches.py`, `FULL_TAIL`): glossy-warm painterly anime gacha key art, adult fashion pin-up styling, a per-heroine figure sentence (`Adult woman with grown-woman proportions, N heads tall (H cm, descriptor), legs, a mature adult face and figure, a defined waist`, filled from the body table), direct teasing gaze, lacquer-like highlights on hair, satin and sheer legwear, warm rim light, saturated colours with a slightly softened background, "Garments and legwear cover the chest, hips and groin and stay exactly in place", adult mature face, one single character, no text, no letters or numerals, no logos, no watermark.

Order of a prompt: `<<<element id>>> is <name>, an adult woman of <age>, <role>. Hair / Eyes / Skin / Face / Signature items / Body. Outfit: ... Pose: ... Expression: ... Framing: ... Setting: ... accent-colour line, tail`. Identity comes from the Element; do not also pass the Element in `medias`.

Tested with `gpt_image_2_6` (1k medium, 9:16 or 2:3): the generator did not refuse or soften any of: thigh-high stockings with lace tops and garter straps, plunging keyhole and heart-cutout cheongsam, bikini with sheer shirt, bunny leotard with pantyhose, silk robe-dress with a mini dress under it, fishnet thigh-highs, latex-look bodysuit, reclining and leaning and kneeling poses, wink and tongue-out wink. 0 moderation refusals in 75 images (51 in round 1, 24 in round 2). Fashion vocabulary was enough; the cut still reads clearly bold.

Learned: a "Skin: ... not tanned" clause fixes Nova's skin drift from P50; "thigh-up in the lower 55%" works for the board screen but the model fills the empty upper field with background decor on some seeds; a very bold cut can drift above the cut the prompt asks for when a reference image is attached.

## 7. Look gate for Full frames

A frame passes when all hold:

1. Boldness: stockings or thigh-high legwear with a band or lace top (or the slot's equivalent: swimwear, bunny, robe), a deep neckline or bare shoulders, and a playful pose from section 3. A frame that could appear in the P50 contact sheet fails.
2. Hard lines (section 2): mature adult face, head count equal to the body table of the heroine (never below 6.75), covered chest and groin, no text, no school coding.
3. Identity: face, hair and signature items match the canon card and the Element (at least 4 of 5 face traits; `hair_never` rules).
4. Anatomy: hands five fingers and no fusions, legs and feet coherent, crossed legs unambiguous, boots and heels with correct count.
5. Accent colour of the heroine at most 20% of the frame (strict mask in `tools/art/golden_gate.py`); gold only as an accent.
6. No text, logo or watermark glyphs anywhere; tiles blank.
7. Metadata scrubbed (`tools/art/scrub_metadata.py`).
